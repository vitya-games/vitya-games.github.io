Privacy Policy

Grill Sitter does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Effective date: August 17, 2026

The English text of this policy is the authoritative version; the Hungarian and Pirate English versions are provided for convenience.

Data stored on your device

The app stores only your best cooked score locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to Vitya Games or to any third party. You can remove it by deleting the app or clearing its local app data.

Accounts, analytics, advertising, and purchases

Grill Sitter:

  • does not require or provide user accounts;
  • does not use analytics or advertising SDKs;
  • does not include advertising;
  • does not offer in-app purchases;
  • does not track users across apps or websites; and
  • does not access location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data.

Children’s privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released.
