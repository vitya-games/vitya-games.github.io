Support

Need help with Grill Sitter? Start with the controls below or contact Vitya Games directly.

How to play

  • Play in landscape orientation.
  • Wait while the grill smoulders; the coals show the stored charge.
  • Tap anywhere on the screen to light a flare.
  • The flare height depends on how much charge you saved.
  • A new flare cannot start until the current one burns out.
  • Cook every hotdog and seagull before it passes the grill.

High score and offline play

Your best cooked score is stored locally on your device. The game requires no account and can be played without an internet connection.

Troubleshooting

  • If the screen asks you to rotate the device, turn it to landscape.
  • If there is no sound, check the device volume and silent-mode setting.
  • If the app stops responding, close it and open it again.
