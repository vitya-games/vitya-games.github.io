Support

Need help with Hotdog Runner? Start with the controls below or contact Vitya Games directly.

How to play

  • Play in landscape orientation.
  • Tap the right half of the screen to jump.
  • Hold the left half of the screen to duck.
  • Jump over grills and duck beneath middle-lane seagulls.

High score and offline play

Your high score is stored locally on your device. The game requires no account and can be played without an internet connection.

Troubleshooting

  • If the screen asks you to rotate the device, turn it to landscape.
  • If there is no sound, check the device volume and silent-mode setting.
  • If the app stops responding, close it and open it again.
