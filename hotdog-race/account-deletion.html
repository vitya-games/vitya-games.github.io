Delete your account

Deleting is permanent and it is not the only option — a suspension pauses an account and can be undone. This page covers both, and says exactly what a deletion removes.

How to request it

  1. Write to support@vityalabs.com from the e-mail address on the account. If you only ever signed in with Google or Game Center, write from any address and tell us the display name on your hotdog and which sign-in you use — we will confirm it is yours before we touch anything.
  2. Put “delete my Hotdog Race account” in the subject line, so the request is not mistaken for a support question.
  3. We confirm by e-mail before deleting, because it cannot be undone afterwards.
  4. The account is deleted within 30 days of that confirmation, and usually much sooner.

A deletion is refused while you are in a race that has not finished. Persisting a race result needs its whole roster of ten, so removing a seat mid-race would void the finish of the other nine. Leave the race, or wait for it to end, and the request goes through.

What a deletion removes

  • Your profile: display name, colours and equipped accessory.
  • Every sign-in attached to the account — the e-mail address, the stored password hash, and every linked Google or platform identity.
  • Every live session, so the account is signed out everywhere immediately.
  • Your wallet, its full ledger, your inventory and everything in it.
  • Your own race results and your seats in past matches.

Everything above is removed in a single database transaction: it either all goes or none of it does. Deleting the app from your device is a separate step and removes the local cache, the stored session token and your key bindings.

What is deliberately kept

  • A record that a deletion happened, when, and why. It is kept without your account identifier, and it exists because a deletion nobody can account for six weeks later is indistinguishable from a bug.
  • The other nine racers’ results from races you took part in. The race happened, and it is not yours to take away from them.
  • Purchase records, for as long as accounting and consumer-law obligations require them to be kept.

A pause instead of an ending

If you only want to stop for a while, ask for a suspension instead. It signs the account out everywhere and keeps it out of races, but your name, your hotdog, your balances and everything you own are still there when you ask for it back. Deleting is for an account that has to stop existing; it cannot be reversed, and credits and items are not refunded.
