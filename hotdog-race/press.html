Short description

Hotdog Race is a ten-racer online party platformer. Press RACE and a cross-play queue seals within ten seconds — any seat still open is filled by a bot, so a race always starts. Run, jump, crouch, slide and use your way past rotating sweepers, crouch gates, shuttle tunnels, diving seagulls and open pits, with the whole field on screen and a dedicated server keeping the finish order honest.

Long description

Hotdog Race starts from a small, stubborn idea: a party race should not need a party to organise. There is one button. Press it and you are in a queue that closes after at most ten seconds, in a race that seats exactly ten, on a course chosen for you. Nobody sends an invite, nobody waits in a lobby for a tenth player, and nobody sits out because their friends are asleep.

The verbs are deliberately small — run, jump, crouch, slide, use — so the difficulty lives in the course rather than in the controls. Rotating sweepers ask for timing, low gates ask for a slide, shuttle tunnels punish greed, and a seagull crossing the whole course from the finish side has to be jumped or ducked depending on which lane it picked. Falling in a pit is three seconds and a respawn, not an ending: the field is still ahead of you and there is still time to catch it.

Every official race is simulated by a dedicated server with no player of its own, so there is no host and no host advantage. Finish order, respawn timing, knockouts and rewards are decided in one place, and a client cannot report a result it did not race. Empty seats are bots rather than a smaller race, which means the ten-slot shape is the same whether nine strangers turned up or none did.

Beside the main event there is a two-player co-op mode built around obstacles nobody clears alone — swing ropes, two-lever doors, sticky sauce that needs a teammate to pull you out of, and a shopping cart that takes two riders — and an offline practice mode with the whole course list, nine bots, no clock and no account, so the game is playable the moment it finishes installing.

Features

  • Ten racers in every start, on every platform, in one shared queue.
  • A queue that closes in ten seconds, with bots filling any seat still open.
  • Six race courses plus twelve practice rooms, every one of them authored by hand.
  • Dedicated-server authority: no host, no host advantage, no client-reported results.
  • One account across every device, with e-mail, Google or platform sign-in.
  • A hotdog you build: sausage, bun and condiment colours free, accessories earned or bought.
  • A two-player co-op mode where neither of you finishes without the other.
  • Offline practice against nine bots, with no account and no rewards.
  • No advertising, no analytics SDK, no cross-app tracking, no chat and no voice.
